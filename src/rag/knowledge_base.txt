E-COMMERCE COMPANY KNOWLEDGE BASE

ORDER POLICY

Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped.
Once an order has been shipped, cancellation may not be possible.
Customers should contact customer support for cancellation requests.

DELIVERY POLICY

Standard delivery usually takes between 3 and 7 business days.
Delivery estimates may vary depending on the customer's location,
seller processing time, weekends and public holidays.

If an order is delivered after the estimated delivery date,
the order may be considered delayed.

RETURN POLICY

Customers can request a return according to the return policy
associated with the product.

Products that are damaged, defective or incorrect may qualify
for return depending on the applicable policy.

Some products may not be eligible for return.

REFUND POLICY

Refunds are generally processed after the returned product has
been received and inspected.

The time required for the refund to reach the customer's account
may depend on the payment method and financial institution.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Customers should provide their order ID when contacting support
about an order-related issue.

For questions about a specific order, the order tools should be
used instead of relying only on general company knowledge.

IMPORTANT AGENT RULE

General company policies should be retrieved from the knowledge
base.

Specific order status, customer information, delivery dates and
order history must be retrieved using the appropriate database
tools.

The agent must never invent information that is not available
from the tools or knowledge base.
